# Brand Discovery Skill

Purpose: prevent premature styling by establishing brand truth first.

## Use
Invoke during `00_intake` and before strategy is allowed through the next ICM gate.

## Required outputs
- audience: demographics, psychographics, jobs, context
- category and competitive frame
- single brand promise
- differentiation / zag
- proof and evidence sources
- protected items, hard bans, constraints
- voice/tone clues
- unresolved questions

## Method
1. Ask one high-value question at a time when human input is required.
2. Separate VERIFIED, CLIENT-STATED, INFERRED, and UNKNOWN facts.
3. Do not invent metrics, testimonials, awards, cultural claims, or customer evidence.
4. Prefer the smallest sufficient discovery packet; do not crawl unrelated project history.
5. Readiness must meet the current ICM gate before strategy begins.

## Handoff
Write only to the assigned intake outputs. End with a handoff packet to strategy containing source pointers, constraints, open questions, and evidence status.
