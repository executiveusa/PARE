# PARÉ — Proof-First Go-to-Market

## Position

**PARÉ**

**One Studio. Infinite possibilities.**

AI changes constantly. Your company should not have to.

PARÉ gives a company one owner-controlled environment for projects, files, agents and infrastructure while letting it use the models and coding agents that fit each job.

Core thesis:

> Intelligence can be rented. The work should remain yours.

## Who we sell to first
Founder-led small and midsize organizations with fragmented digital systems and expensive coordination:
- agencies and creative studios;
- local businesses;
- nonprofits;
- coaches/creators;
- e-commerce operators;
- small internal marketing/product teams.

Do not lead with “agent framework.” Lead with fewer tools, clearer ownership, faster verified outcomes and less operational dependence on vendors.

## Commercial ladder

### 1. Vibe Audit
Paid diagnosis of website, brand, search, social, funnel, technology, automation and AI opportunities.

Goal: cash before custom build. Produce an evidence-backed owner brief and ranked opportunity map.

### 2. Rescue Sprint
Bounded implementation of the highest-value problem found in the audit. Loop Engineering supplies the execution/verification contract.

### 3. Sovereign Launch
Deliver the site/app, repository, project intelligence, agent access, automation, deployment, documentation and owner-controlled infrastructure.

### 4. MACS Ops
Recurring operation through MACS Digital Media: content, campaigns, websites, analytics, automations, maintenance and agentic execution. MACS / Agent Max is a separate business identity and does not inherit the owner’s personal Hermes authority.

Pricing remains a commercial test, not product truth. Track gross margin and revenue produced per human hour before locking tiers.

## Proof case #1 — MACS Digital Media
The first public case study is not “we made an AI platform.”

It is:

> How a small digital media company can operate with enterprise-level AI leverage while keeping its projects, files and infrastructure portable.

Demo one real flow:
1. a client request enters PARÉ;
2. PARÉ loads the project context;
3. a real provider/agent is selected;
4. the job executes through the sovereign runtime;
5. artifacts appear in the project;
6. Loop Engineering verifies the result;
7. the same project is reachable through API/MCP/CLI;
8. MACS keeps the durable state.

No fake model response or fabricated outcome in the case study.

## 30-day proof loop

### Days 0–2 — ship and prove
- reach `PREVIEW VERIFIED`;
- owner production approval;
- reach `PRODUCTION VERIFIED`;
- capture real Studio + provider + diffusion + project persistence demo;
- publish PARÉ homepage/Journal;
- publish MACS proof case.

### Week 1 — sell audits
- direct outreach to 5–10 qualified businesses;
- sell paid Vibe Audits;
- record objections and repeated system pain;
- no custom platform features unless a paid delivery exposes a repeated need.

### Week 2 — rescue
- convert strongest audits into bounded Rescue Sprints;
- measure turnaround, human hours and business impact;
- encode repeated diagnosis/execution into PARÉ skills.

### Week 3 — sovereign launch
- convert suitable clients into owner-controlled deployments;
- standardize deployment, secret, ICM and handoff contracts.

### Week 4 — recurring operations
- convert launch clients to MACS Ops where ongoing operation is valuable;
- publish verified case studies;
- use the evidence to improve Product Hunt/marketplace positioning.

## North-star operating metric

**Verified revenue delivered per human hour.**

Supporting metrics:
- audit-to-rescue conversion;
- rescue-to-launch conversion;
- launch-to-recurring conversion;
- median time from request to verified deliverable;
- agent/runtime cost per verified deliverable;
- rollback/failure rate;
- percentage of repeated work encoded as reusable skills.

## Admission rule
A new framework/product enters the active path only if it materially strengthens at least one of:
- PARÉ capability/reliability;
- revenue/distribution;
- reusable intelligence;
- owner sovereignty/security;
- Loop/Gauntlet verification.

Otherwise: **PARK**.
