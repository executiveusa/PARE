# PARÉ Content Ops — OffBrand Daily Topic Brief

Status: approval-gated editorial research workflow

## Source

Primary inspiration source:

- https://offbrandkoto.substack.com/archive

The source is for **topic discovery, pattern recognition, framing inspiration, and strategic contrast**. It is not a source for copying article text, distinctive phrasing, proprietary frameworks, or paywalled material.

## Daily job

Run once per day at **1:00 AM local owner time**.

1. Review the latest OffBrand/Koto Substack archive entries and any newly published post.
2. Identify the strongest topic that can be made relevant to The Pauli Effect / PARÉ audience.
3. Prefer topics that connect branding, creative direction, positioning, design systems, AI-assisted creative work, agent workflows, ownership/sovereignty, small-business execution, nonprofit/community brands, or practical operating lessons.
4. Do not merely summarize the source. Find a distinct Pauli angle that is useful to our audience.
5. Apply PARÉ Humanize / anti-AI-prose rules before delivery:
   - remove generic AI throat-clearing, filler, inflated claims, canned contrasts, and repetitive triads;
   - use concrete nouns, real operators, practical situations, and plain language;
   - preserve uncertainty and never invent data, quotes, examples, or source claims;
   - keep the result specific and useful rather than impressive-sounding.
6. Produce an **agent-usable brief for approval**, not a finished article.
7. Never publish automatically. Human approval is required before drafting/publishing.

## Audience

Primary audience:

- founders and owner-operators;
- small businesses and nonprofits;
- creative operators and brand teams;
- nontechnical owners using AI/agents to get real work done;
- teams interested in owning their software, workflows, files, and brand intelligence rather than depending entirely on SaaS platforms.

## Required brief format

### Topic of the day
A clear working title in Pauli language.

### Why this matters to our audience
2–4 sentences connecting the topic to a real business/brand/operator problem.

### Source inspiration
- article/post title;
- source URL;
- publication date if available;
- 2–4 sentence paraphrase of the source idea;
- no long quotes or copied passages.

### Our angle
Explain how our take differs from the source. The brief must add a Pauli perspective, practical operator lesson, or brand/agent implication.

### Core argument
One sentence that the eventual article should prove.

### 3–5 supporting points
Short bullets that can become sections or examples.

### Relatable examples
2–3 concrete situations drawn from our audience: small business, nonprofit, local operator, creative team, or agent-assisted workflow. Mark hypothetical examples as hypothetical.

### Practical takeaway
What the reader should understand, decide, or do differently after reading.

### Suggested headline options
3 options maximum. Avoid clickbait and generic AI language.

### Suggested CTA
One soft, relevant CTA tied to PARÉ / The Pauli Effect / working with us. No hard sell unless the topic naturally calls for it.

### Approval status
Always finish with:

`STATUS: DRAFT BRIEF — HUMAN APPROVAL REQUIRED BEFORE ARTICLE DRAFT OR PUBLISHING`

## Selection rules

Prefer the single strongest idea over a list of weak ideas.

Reject a source/topic when:

- it depends on copying unique source language;
- it is too agency-insider-specific to matter to our audience;
- it cannot be connected to a real operator problem;
- the source evidence is too thin or inaccessible;
- the best angle would require inventing facts or pretending we have experience we do not have.

If nothing strong is available that day, say so and return the best fallback topic from the recent archive, clearly labeled as a fallback.

## Approval gate

This workflow ends at the brief. It does not draft, schedule, post, or publish automatically.

Human approval can then trigger a separate workflow to:

`approve topic -> draft -> humanize -> fact/source check -> brand/SEO pass -> final approval -> publish`
